Experiences

Bachelor's Thesis (HiPeRT Lab)

During my bachelor's thesis I had the pleasure of working in the HiPeRT Lab, where I worked with technologies related to:

  • Robotics
  • Autonomous vehicles
  • Remote driving systems
  • IoT technologies in the context of self-driving cars

During that experience I learned how to integrate a complete autonomous vehicle stack on an AgileX Hunter 2 rover, and gained hands-on experience with ROS, C++, and embedded integrations.

My final work compared how the Robot Operating System (ROS) and MQTT perform in remote driving scenarios. I implemented a bridge to enable communication between different ROS instances using MQTT.

Roboracer

After my thesis I continued working with the HiPeRT Lab on the Roboracer project.

This competition allows students and private participants to build a 1/10‑scale autonomous car and conduct research on autonomous vehicles, embedded systems, robotics, optimization, and related algorithms.

Personally, I mainly worked on perception (how the robot perceives the world) and on tooling.

In June 2026 I had the opportunity and the pleasure to participate in the 27th Roboracer competition, held at the ICRA conference in Vienna, Austria.
